Alhamdolillah Ehsaas Foundation spent independence day with the beautiful people of Jala bela and Agra in Peshawar, the children sang dil dil Pakistan with us and waved their flags high in the sky as we all shouted Pakistan Zindabad!

We distributed 200 food packets, 150 medical kits, 4 boxes of clothes and 250 plastic chappals in 4 various sizes, and a few bottles of water (luckily they already have some access to water) 

Special thanks to Sahibzada Mohammed Pervaiz, District Food Inspector Sahibzada Ziad, Asadullah, Zubair Bhai, the Agra Council www.agracouncil.blogspot.com, the unnamed princess from Chitral and sweet Subhan Baba (for the gurh ka sharbat) who assisted us in distributing the goods in such a systematic manner due to their lists and tokens and staunch patience, perseverence, sheer sophistication and strong spirit in the face of the terrible destruction that they have suffered.

In future we would like to supply them with gas cylinders, washing powder, soap, masalas, and other ration items, excluding atta which they say they have enough of. These 2 neighbouring villages support each other so nicely that they redirect goods to each other whenever there is an excess of anything. how refreshing! 

We will be keeping a close eye on our new friends in Jala bela and Agra - This was Ehsaas Foundation's second visit to them in 4 days. Yesterday was the first time I have ever been to Peshawar in my life, and I want to go there again and again Inshallah, such troubled times yet these people have such immense hospitality that Sahibzada Mohammed Pervaiz  took us to his ruined home to share their simple aftaari, and would barely let us leave, we had to rush because we wanted to see the extent of the destruction further in while there was still some light...the damage was bad.

In the meantime please please tell us where to go next, msf and other groups are taking great care of these places since the last 3 days, so we would like to go to ANY place within a 5hr driving radius of Islamabad that needs help more urgently, am horrified by reports of people eating LEAVES in some region.

How can we coordinate better to get reccy on the ground? We have a limited amount of resources for food items that we want to distribute ASAP.

I am concerned about the issue of overlapping aid in some parts, and a total absence in others, please keep in touch and continue to share your comments and suggestions. Remember information is power. Please share it. Every second counts.

Wishing you love, luck and strength 


We have created a medical kit with 11 items approved locally by Dr. Sehr Zulfiqar, and internationally by Dr. Ahmed Mukhtar of Doctors Without Borders from Brussells It costed approx 125 rupees, and served a family.

Primordin (povodine, iodine 7.50% topical antiseptic germicide 50ml
Unetox-T (Attapulgite BP) 500mg 10 tablets
Benadryl Diphenhydramine cough syrup 120 ml
Chloramphenicol 0.5% eye drops 15ml
Noveniphenicol Chloramphenicol eye ointment 1% 3.5 gms
Surgical bandage BP type II
Calamine lotion 100ml
2 ORS sachets that make one litre for rehydration drink
Disprin Soluble Aspirin 10 tablets
Absorbent cotton wool 200g and one ladys panty underwear for menstrual protection

separately btw aqua water tabs available for 75rs for 20tablet pack
