Ehsaas — compassion, caring and empathy

“If you teach a person how to fish, you feed them for a lifetime.” We aim to create a self-sustained community culture whereby people help themselves and each other. Our school serves the underprivileged katchi abadi and provides a modern, fully facilitated educational institution and campus approved to become a college. We have over 400 girls enrolled at present.

Creating Ehsaas

Ehsaas Foundation was founded in 1998 and has been officially registered since 18 March 2006 under the Voluntary Social Welfare Agencies (Registration and Control) Ordinance 1961.

We built our school building from scratch and serve children from the severely impoverished area of Golra. Many of our children study free or for an incredibly nominal fee thanks to our generous Pakistani donors here and abroad.

We teach the government curriculum along with a good old-fashioned dose of the basics: reading, writing and arithmetic, while encouraging thinking and questioning. Along the way, the children become bright-eyed students with sparkling uniforms, a healthy perspective on life and a bag full of hope and joy.

We pride ourselves on our multi-faith staff, students and celebrations.

Founder & President Mrs. Rifat Shaukat

M.Ed. Education   •   M.A. Urdu Literature   •   B.Sc. Biochemistry

Mrs. Rifat Shaukat began Ehsaas Foundation School with a handful of children she discovered picking trash in the streets. Drawing on five decades of teaching experience in Pakistan and the United Kingdom, she set out to transform the lives of impoverished children, primarily in Golra Sharif.

Her career began at Azim Garrison, Lahore, teaching science. She later taught at schools in London for 25 years before returning to Pakistan and establishing Ehsaas Foundation School in 1998.

Since an early age Mrs. Shaukat wrote for college publications and Urdu digests. In London she continued to write for Mashriq, Asia and Akhbar-e-Watan, and worked alongside Ashfaq Ahmed Sahib and Mahmood Hashmi Sahib as a political columnist and writer.

Following the 2005 earthquake, Ehsaas Foundation received recognition from the Capital Development Authority and a letter of appreciation from a Pakistan Army unit for relief delivery and distribution. The Foundation also supported flood relief in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa in 2010.

Principal Ms. Talat Qureshi

Ms. Talat Qureshi earned her Master’s degree in Psychology from Jamia Millia College, Karachi, and has more than a decade of corporate-sector experience in London. She has dedicated fifteen years of service as Principal of Ehsaas Foundation School. We owe much to her dedication, hard work and grace under pressure.
