Ehsaas - to have a feeling of compassion and empathy

 "If you teach a person how to fish , you feed them for a lifetime" We aim to create a self sustained community culture whereby people help themselves and each other. Our School serves the underprivileged katchi abadi (slum areas) and provides a modern fully facilitated educational institution and campus which has now been approved by the authorities to become a College. We have 750 girls and boys till FSc level at present.

Creating Ehsaas
Ehsaas Foundation founded in 1998 has been OFFICIALLY registered since 18th March 2006 under the Voluntary Social Welfare Agencies (Registration and Control) Ordinance 1961.  

We have built our school building from scratch. We serve over 750 children from the severely impoverished area Golra. Many of our children study free or for an incredibly nominal fee thanks to our generous Pakistani donors from here and abroad. 

We teach the government curriculum, along with a good old fashioned dose of basics: reading, writing and arithmetic, and some thinking and questioning hopefully. Golra is a whole other world than Islamabad  - first we have to teach them how to wash and bathe.

And then somehow along the way they turn into bright eyed, beautiful, (incredibly rambunctious) lovely little students with perfect sparkling uniforms and a healthy perspective on life with a bag full of hope and joy, did I mention that sometimes they are trouble? You can come over and ask our cucumber cool Principal Ms. Talat Qureshi, or you can ask the children themselves who will of course deny it firmly despite the twinkle in their eyes...

Founder & President Mrs. Rifat Shaukat

M.Ed. Education

M.A. Urdu Literature

B.sc. Biochemistry

​​Mrs. Rifat Shaukat began running Ehsaas Foundation School by starting with just a handful of children that she discovered were picking trash in the streets. Now having teaching experience since five decades in Pakistan and the United Kingdom she decided to try and turn around the lives of some impoverished children, primarily residing in the Golra Sharif area of Islamabad.

Mrs. Rifat Shaukat’s career began starting at Azim Garrison, Lahore teaching science and then teaching at various schools in London, England for 25 years and then returning to Pakistan and establishing Ehsaas Foundation School in 1998.

Since an early age Mrs. Rifat Shaukat used to write for her college publications at Lahore College and Punjab University, and in the Urdu digest. She also earned certificates in swordmanship, rifle club, doll making, cooking and flower arrangement.

In London she continued to write for Mashriq, Asia, and Akhbar e Watan; and had the opportunity to work alongside Ashfaq Ahmed Sahib, and Mahmood Hashmi Sahib as a political columnist, as well as writing for the womens page and childrens stories.

In 1998 Mrs. Rifat Shaukat began Ehsaas Foundation School by seeking out street children and introducing them to a regular school environment.  Currently the school is educating over 750 children in Golra Sharif, It also has a free health clinic.

After the earthquake of 2005 Ehsaas Foundation received awards from the Capital Development Authority for its disaster relief victims services in the Tent Villages of Sector H Islamabad Headed by Mr. Kamran Lashari. In 2010 Ehsaas Foundation also provided flood relief delivery & distribution services in Khyber Pakhtoonkhwa (Nowshera Charsadda and Peshawar) within it’s limited capacity. 

Ehsaas Foundation also received a letter of Appreciation from the Pakistan Army Unit at Garhi Habibullah regarding Relief Goods Deliveries & Distribution for Earthquake 2005.

Principal Ms. Talat Qureshi

Ms. Talat Qureshi acquired her Masters degree in Psychology from Jamia Milia College Karachi and has over a decade of experience in the corporate sector in London, U.K. Ms. Qureshi has spent fifteen years service as Ehsaas Foundation School Principal. We owe everything to her immense dedication, hard work and grace under pressure.
