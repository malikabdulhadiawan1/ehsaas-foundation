Our Projects

Ehsaas Foundation supports education, practical skills, healthcare and community welfare in Golra and surrounding communities.

Sewing School

We empower young women by training them to stitch clothes professionally. This practical skill enables them to pursue work from their own homes. Our sewing teacher carefully instructs students in cutting, stitching and decorative techniques.

Tarlai Sewing Project

In 1997 we donated sewing machines to train a group of local girls in the village of Tarlai. After completing a year-long course, they learned sewing, cutting and embroidery, providing many with a reliable source of income.

Medical Centre

Our doctor is available for consultations with women and children in the Golra region. We have also held medical camps where needed. Our mobile medical centre brings a nurse, doctor and medicinal supplies directly to local communities.

Chinol Well Project

Safe drinking water was desperately needed in the village of Chinol. We were able to provide this essential facility by digging a well there in 1998.

Restored Eyesight

These three children were blind from birth. In 1999, their eyesight was restored through hospitalization and operations, allowing them to continue their education at school.

Ehsaas Village

A home for women who survived the 2005 earthquake, where residents can live, study and work on the premises of Ehsaas Foundation in a wheelchair-accessible environment.
