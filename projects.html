Sewing School
We empower young women by training them how to stitch clothes professionally. Acquiring this strong skill enables them to pursue a lucrative line of work from the comfort of their own homes.Our sewing teacher carefully instructs them in the art of cuts and stitching as well as decorative motifs.

Tarlai Sewing Project
In 1997 we donated the sewing machines for the training of a group of local girls in the village of Tarlai. After a complete years course, they learned sewing, cutting and embroidery, providing many with a solid source of income.


Medical Centre
Our doctor is available for consultation by women and children in the Golra region. In the past we have also held medical camps where needed.Our mobile medical centre consists of a nurse, doctor and medicinal supplies taken door to door by one of the Ehsaas vans.


Chinol Well Project
Drinking water was desperately required in the village of Chinol. We were able to provide that facility by digging a well there in 1998.


Restored Eyesight
These three children were blind since birth. In 1999, their eyesight was restored through hospitalization and operations. Currently they are all successfully studying at school.

Ehsaas Village
​ 
Present day. A Home for earthquake 2005 survivor ladies who live, study and work on the premises of Ehsaas Foundation in a wheelchair accessible compound called Ehsaas Village.
