​​Ehsaas Foundation School in Golra Sharif, Islamabad, is a bi-lingual (Urdu/English) learning centre for over 600 underprivileged children in the surrounding katchi abadi area.They range in ages from 5 – 15, and are taught by exceptional teachers. Many Ehsaas students are provided free schooling and uniforms, since many cannot afford to pay.

We have just been approved by the City's Educational Authorities to become Ehsaas Foundation College as of November 2016.
